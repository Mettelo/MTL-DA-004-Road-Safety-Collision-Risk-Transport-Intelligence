# Required Deliverables

Every team repository must contain evidence for all deliverables below.

## D1 — Discovery & Data Quality Assessment
**Due:** End of Week 1  
**Location:** `docs/01-discovery/`

Required:
- source-file inventory;
- row counts;
- grain of collisions, vehicles and casualties;
- key relationships;
- duplicate/missing checks;
- year coverage;
- coded-variable review;
- data-quality limitations;
- initial analytical risks.

## D2 — Integrated Data Model & Transformation Layer
**Due:** Initial version by end of Week 3  
**Location:** `src/`, `sql/`, `docs/02-data-model/`

Required:
- preserved raw-data approach;
- reproducible transformations;
- collision-vehicle-casualty relationship model;
- documented join logic;
- severity/KSI derivations;
- code/value mapping approach;
- reproducible build instructions.

## D3 — KPI & Metric Catalogue
**Due:** End of Week 3  
**Location:** `docs/03-kpi-catalogue/`

Expected metric areas:
- collisions;
- casualties;
- fatalities;
- serious casualties;
- KSI;
- casualties by road-user type;
- vehicle involvement;
- hotspot measures;
- severity distribution;
- temporal distribution.

Each metric must include definition, calculation, grain, source fields, exclusions and limitations.

## D4 — Geographic & Hotspot Analysis
**Due:** End of Week 4  
**Location:** `analysis/` and `docs/04-geographic-analysis/`

Required:
- geographic concentration;
- persistent patterns across years;
- documented hotspot methodology;
- mapping outputs;
- limitations of location-based interpretation.

## D5 — Vulnerable Road User Analysis
**Due:** End of Week 5  
**Location:** `analysis/` and `docs/05-vulnerable-road-users/`

Investigate:
- pedestrians;
- pedal cyclists;
- motorcyclists;
- other relevant vulnerable groups.

Include severity, demographics, time, road environment and contextual patterns.

## D6 — Collision Circumstance & Severity Analysis
**Due:** End of Week 5  
**Location:** `analysis/` and `docs/06-severity-analysis/`

Assess patterns relating to:
- road type;
- junction detail;
- speed environment;
- lighting;
- weather;
- vehicle type;
- time period;
- casualty characteristics.

Avoid presenting association as causation.

## D7 — Management Dashboard / Decision-Support Product
**Due:** Draft Week 6; final Week 7  
**Location:** `dashboard/`

Should support:
- headline road-safety KPIs;
- KSI monitoring;
- geographic exploration;
- vulnerable-road-user analysis;
- temporal patterns;
- collision circumstances.

## D8 — Technical Handover
**Due:** Final week  
**Location:** `docs/07-technical-handover/`

Must explain:
- repository structure;
- source-data acquisition;
- coded-value mappings;
- transformation process;
- data model;
- metric definitions;
- reproduction steps;
- known limitations;
- extension recommendations.

## D9 — Executive Briefing
**Due:** Final week  
**Location:** `deliverables/executive-briefing/`

Maximum two pages or equivalent.

## D10 — Final Presentation
**Due:** Mettelo Demo Day  
**Location:** `deliverables/presentation/`

15–20 minute presentation plus Q&A.

## D11 — Contribution Record
**Due:** Final submission  
**Location:** `CONTRIBUTIONS.md`

For each member:
- role;
- responsibilities;
- main deliverables;
- linked evidence;
- presentation contribution.
