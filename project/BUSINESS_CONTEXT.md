# Business Context

## Client Profile

**Client:** Confidential Regional Transport & Road Safety Authority  
**Sector:** Public Sector / Transport  
**Primary Concern:** Reducing serious road harm and improving evidence-led intervention

## Why This Engagement Was Commissioned

A recent strategic review identified several weaknesses in the organisation's current road-safety reporting:

1. collision, vehicle and casualty datasets are not consistently analysed together;
2. reporting focuses heavily on counts rather than severity and exposure context;
3. hotspot work is not always reproduced consistently across teams;
4. vulnerable-road-user analysis is fragmented;
5. coded variables are interpreted inconsistently;
6. management needs clearer evidence to support prioritisation of limited intervention resources.

## Stakeholders

### Director of Transport Safety & Strategy — Project Sponsor
Needs a concise evidence base for strategic prioritisation.

### Head of Road Safety
Needs visibility of KSI patterns, persistent hotspots and high-risk circumstances.

### Network Operations Lead
Needs intelligence on road type, junction, lighting, speed environment and operational patterns.

### Active Travel Lead
Needs analysis of pedestrians, cyclists and other vulnerable road users.

### Policy & Evaluation Lead
Needs careful distinction between association, risk context and causal claims.

### Data & Insight Lead
Needs reproducible logic, documented code mappings and a solution suitable for handover.

## Decision Context

The organisation is considering where to focus future road-safety activity, including:

- engineering investigation;
- speed-management work;
- junction review;
- vulnerable-road-user interventions;
- targeted communications;
- further research;
- enhanced monitoring.

The analysis should support prioritisation, not automatically prescribe interventions.
